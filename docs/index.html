Who am I?

Hello!


My name is Dylan, I am a game and level designer. I recently finished my Bachelors in Video Game Design at Birmingham City University


I have experience leading large teams of up to 70 as well as smaller teams of 2-6 throughout my time at university, playing a key role in defining gameplay loops, mechanics and the direction the team will move in giving me a lot of knowledge not only regarding how to lead a team but also in general game design!


I also have experience using Unity and Unreal Engine 5. I am knowledgeable on how to create levels from a technical POV using Unity's probuilder and Unreals modelling tool and blueprint system. I have a strong grasp of level design theory, pacing, signposting and everything else you need to be a good level designer!


I'm a very emotion based designer, I believe games are an extremely important art form that we should use as a way to express emotion and deeper intrinsic meanings to players, helping them connect with what we create. I place a heavy emphasis on how I can use the formal mechanics/systems of games to convey and further its themes or target emotion.

Open Your Heart

Juniper Dev "Very Serious" Game Jam




Roles: Game Designer, Level Designer, UI Designer, Narrative Designer

Team size: 2

Engine: Godot

Design Software: Confluence

Genre: Adventure, Puzzle

Project duration: 7 Days

Link to the project: Open Your Heart Itch Page

Discover love, kindness and the way to open you heart!

Open Your Heart is a 3rd person adventure puzzle game about helping others and spinning open the tight valve that has sealed your heart! Inspired by games like A Short Hike and Pikuniku you must help those in need by using your spinning ability to solve puzzles, discovering love, kindness and the way to open Your heart! This game was made as part of Juniper Devs "Very Serious" 2026 game jam with the theme "Spin to Win" in mind! Full gameplay walkthrough can be found here.

I was responsible for all of the level design, UI design, gameplay mechanic design and narrative design. Due to the short length of the game jam it was a challenge to condense the narrative and gameplay mechanics into something that was coherent and consistent with the game jam theme. To aid in this I utilised the "Design by subtraction" philosophy extensively to accurately remove the least essential elements of the game and ensure the project remained feasible. Alongside this I made extensive use of production based management strategies like MoSCoW lists and "Epics and user stories" to outline exactly what needed developing, modelling etc to increase team cohesion and limit any confusion with regards to what to focus on next

ReMove It

Game Jam




Roles: Overall Designer, Developer and Artist

Team size: 1 (solo project)

Engine: Unreal Engine 5

Design Software: Miro

Genre: Action, Puzzle, Roguelike

Project duration: 2 Weeks

Link to the project: ReMove It itch page

ReMove It is a short fast paced action roguelike I made during a game jam that I and some friends from University started with the theme of "start with everything end with nothing". Inspired by games like Katamari Damacy, players must suck up as many objects they can each round in an effort to stop their company from going bankrupt. However, after the end of each round the player must choose an ability to remove such as jumping, sprinting and even the User Interface, making the next round more difficult. I used this game jam project to explore and expand my knowledge in areas I felt were lacking such as 3D art (I made the character model and animations for the game from stratch) as well as improving my general Unreal Engine 5 skills by increasing my familiarity with the engine and introducing more complex systems like game instances which I had not previously explored.
