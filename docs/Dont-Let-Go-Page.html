[Don't] Let Go

General Design Lead and Level Designer







Project Overview


Roles: Overall Design Lead, Level Designer, Camera Designer, Narrative Designer

Team size: 6

Engine: Unity

Project duration: 5 months

Link to the project: [Don't] Let Go Itch Page



[Don't] Let Go was the game my team and I created as our Final Major Project at Birmingham City University. The team and I set out to create a game that was around one hour long with the goal of making it as finished and polished as possible, not just a 5 minute typical student demo. On this project I was appointed as the lead designer. This role saw me managing the team and the games design as well as designing much of the narrative, core mechanics and levels. Level Design was a major focus for me throughout the project, I designed, whiteboxed, tested and did all of the environment art/lighting for the levels.

My Responsibilities

- Responsible for managing the design documentation and ensuring the team had a clear understanding of my creative vision/direction


- Responsible for solving team and design conflicts


- Responsible for organising game design documentation


- Responsible for designing, whiteboxing, testing and environment art for all of the levels
